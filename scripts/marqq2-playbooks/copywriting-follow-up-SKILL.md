# Copywriting Follow-up

Write one follow-up that stands alone for a prospect who did not reply.

Rules:
- Never say “just following up” or repeat the first email.
- Introduce one fresh angle: proof, practical insight, objection, or useful resource.
- Keep the message shorter than the first touch.
- Preserve the same grounded personalization and one low-friction CTA.
- Stop or recommend a breakup message after the configured sequence limit.

Return: `subject`, `body`, `angle`, `cta`, and `sendAfterDays`.
